# Cyber Nexa Solution — design plan

## Subject
A technology solutions company (websites, apps, AI, automation, business software) for SMBs.
Brand: Cyber Nexa Solution — technology partner; trust and quality at the core. Job of the site: make a growing business feel this team will
understand their operations and build something dependable. Audience: business owners &
operations managers, not developers. Primary job: turn a visitor into a project inquiry.

## Anti-cliché check (traits I am deliberately avoiding)
- NOT warm cream (#F4F1EA) + serif + terracotta (#D97757). My base is a cool paper with a
  deep petrol-green identity and a spring-teal tech accent — a different family entirely.
- NOT near-black + single acid accent. The hero is a deep *forest-petrol* field, not black.
- NOT broadsheet hairlines / zero radius / newspaper columns.
- NOT one radius + one grey shadow on identical cards: hierarchy varies (elevated hero panel,
  bordered service tiles, borderless feature rows, connective-line dividers).
- NOT template chrome: no tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrows, no "A · B · C" mid-dots, no spaced-em-dash
  labels, no monospace data labels, no literal "→" appended to text. Lucide icons used as real
  elements, sparingly.

## Type
- Display/headings: "Sora" (geometric, precise, engineered feel — trust + technology).
- Body/UI: "Inter". Clearly distinct roles (Sora has geometric character; Inter is the workhorse).
- Scale ~1.25. Tight tracking on large display; normal on body. Line length < 70ch for prose.
- No accent-one-word tricks; headline weight/size/color carry emphasis as a whole.

## Layout
- Hero: left-aligned headline + copy + two CTAs on the left; an animated "System Map" on the
  right — a central "Your business" node linked to AI / Web / Mobile / Cloud / Automation /
  Data / Systems nodes with connective lines and gentle pulses. This is the value proposition
  drawn literally, not decoration. Stacks on mobile (map above? no — copy first, map below).
- The connective-line motif recurs as thin section dividers and behind section headings.
- Sections: services grid, solutions/industries, an approach/"how we work" sequence (numbered —
  legitimately a sequence), sample work, trust band, CTA. Left-aligned section intros.

## Motion (one orchestrated moment)
- On hero load: the System Map draws its links and pops nodes in once (staggered), then settles
  into a slow ambient pulse along the links. Everything else is quiet: hover states + reveal of
  a section only when it enters view, subtle, honoring prefers-reduced-motion (static fallback).

## Admin
- Separate, calmer SaaS surface: Inter throughout, denser, light/dark, brand-green as the single
  accent, real tables/drawers/toasts/skeletons/empty states. Not marketing-flashy.

## Restraint
Boldness spent on the System Map hero. Everything else disciplined: generous whitespace, one
accent color doing the work, minimal shadows (soft, only where elevation is real).
